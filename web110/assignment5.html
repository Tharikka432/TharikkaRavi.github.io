Activity 1: Social Networking Social networking is a way for people and organizations to connect and communicate online. Social media platforms make it easier to stay in touch with friends, family, classmates, and even people from different countries. Some popular social networking sites are Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X (Twitter), and WhatsApp. Out of these platforms, LinkedIn interests me the most because it focuses on professional networking and career opportunities. It allows people to connect with others in their field, learn about jobs, and share their accomplishments. Social networking has changed the way people communicate because it allows information to be shared quickly and helps users stay connected no matter where they are. Even though social media has many benefits, internet security is very important. Internet security refers to protecting information and communication over the internet. Today, cybercriminals use different methods to access people's personal information. Some common threats include spam emails, phishing scams, malware, and identity theft. Phishing is one of the most common online threats. It happens when someone sends a fake email or message that looks legitimate in order to trick people into providing personal information such as passwords, banking information, or account details. This is why users should always be careful when opening emails or clicking links from unknown sources. One important way to keep information safe online is through encryption. Encryption is a security method that converts information into a coded form that can only be read by authorized users. Many encryption systems use two keys: a public key and a private key. The public key can be shared, while the private key remains secret. Together, these keys help protect data and prevent unauthorized access. Learning about internet security is important because it helps people stay safe while using technology and social media. Activity 2: Blogging A blog, which is short for "weblog," is a website that is updated regularly with new content. People use blogs to share opinions, experiences, knowledge, and information about topics that interest them. Blogs can cover just about anything, including travel, technology, education, sports, health, and personal experiences. Blogging is the process of creating and publishing content on a blog. The person who writes the content is called a blogger. One of the reasons blogging has become so popular is that it is easy to get started. Platforms such as WordPress and Blogger provide simple tools that allow people to create blogs without needing advanced coding skills. There are several advantages to blogging. It gives people a place to express their thoughts, share knowledge, and connect with others who have similar interests. Blogs can also help individuals build a personal brand or even earn money through advertisements and sponsorships. In addition, blogging helps improve writing and communication skills. A successful blog can attract a loyal audience and create valuable networking opportunities. Activity 3: The Honeycomb Model The Honeycomb Model is used to explain how people interact on social media. It identifies seven key building blocks that make social media platforms effective and engaging. These building blocks are identity, conversations, sharing, presence, relationships, reputation, and groups. Identity focuses on how people present themselves online. Conversations refer to the interactions users have with one another. Sharing involves exchanging content such as photos, videos, and posts. Presence relates to knowing when others are online or active. The remaining elements are relationships, reputation, and groups. Relationships describe the connections people build online. Reputation refers to how trustworthy and credible a person or organization appears to others. Groups are communities formed around shared interests and goals. I think the Honeycomb Model is useful because it shows the different ways people connect and interact through social media. It also helps businesses understand their audiences and create more effective digital marketing strategies.
