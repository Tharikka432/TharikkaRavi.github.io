Activity 1 Search Engine Optimization (SEO) is the process of improving a website's visibility on search engines such as Google. It plays an important role in ensuring that websites are accessible, well-structured, and easy for users to navigate. SEO should always be user-focused and goal-driven. SEO helps attract visitors by optimizing websites so that they appear higher in search engine results. However, newly launched websites may not appear immediately because search engines need time to crawl and index their content. This process allows Google to understand what the website is about before displaying it in search results. In my view, a user-friendly website structure is one of the most important aspects of SEO because it helps both visitors and search engines access information easily. The ultimate goal of SEO is to provide a positive user experience. Website owners can achieve this by creating engaging content, improving page loading speed, securing websites with HTTPS encryption, and using Google tools to increase online visibility. In addition, sharing valuable information, images, and videos can help attract and retain visitors. Overall, effective SEO benefits both businesses and users by making online content more accessible and useful. Activity 2 Artificial Intelligence (AI) refers to technology designed to mimic human intelligence and perform tasks that typically require human thinking. AI includes several areas, such as machine learning, deep learning, computer vision, robotics, speech recognition, and natural language processing. Machine learning is an important component of AI because it allows computers to learn from data and improve their performance over time without being explicitly programmed for every task. Deep learning further enhances these capabilities by enabling systems to recognize patterns and relationships through multiple layers of processing. One aspect that stood out to me in the article was the discussion of the risks associated with AI. These risks include hallucinations, bias, consent issues, and security concerns. As AI becomes more integrated into everyday life, it is important to address these challenges responsibly. The article recommends several approaches, including explainability, governance and accountability measures, cultural audits, and public education. These strategies can help organizations better understand the potential consequences of AI systems and reduce ethical concerns. The article also discusses ten alternatives to ChatGPT. Each platform has unique strengths. For example, Google Gemini and Microsoft 365 Copilot offer AI-powered assistance and search capabilities, while Claude is known for producing thoughtful conversational responses. Perplexity AI is particularly useful for research because it provides citations for information sources. Other tools, such as Jasper and Writesonic, are designed to support content creation and marketing activities. Overall, the article emphasizes that users should consider factors such as accuracy, creativity, safety, and workflow requirements when selecting an AI chatbot. Activity 3 A search engine is a software program that helps users find information on the internet. Search engines work by scanning websites, collecting data, and organizing that information into searchable indexes. When users enter a query, the search engine analyzes the keywords and retrieves the most relevant results from its database. The article explains how search engines discover and index websites, as well as how they process search queries to identify relevant information. It also highlights the differences between search engines and web directories. Another important point is the effective use of search terms and keywords when looking for information online. I found it interesting that modern search engines offer a variety of advanced features to improve search results. These include Boolean operators, advanced search filters, and specialized search tools that help users locate information more efficiently. Overall, understanding how search engines work can help users conduct more accurate and effective online searches.
