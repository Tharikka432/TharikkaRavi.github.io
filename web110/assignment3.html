Search Engine Optimization (SEO) is the process of improving a websites visibility on search engines like Google. It helps websites attract visitors by making content easier to find, access and understand. Effective SEO focuses on both user experience and website performance. Activity 1 SEO helps websites appear in Google search results and reach users. New websites may not appear immediately because Google needs time to crawl and index them. Website design should be simple and accessible so search engines can easily understand content. The main priority is providing users with the possible experience. Important SEO practices include creating valuable and engaging content improving website speed securing the site with HTTPS and promoting products through Google services. Businesses can also share information, photos, videos and educational content to increase visibility and attract more visitors.
