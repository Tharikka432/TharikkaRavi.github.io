Activity 1 Email is one of the most common ways people communicate today, and Gmail is one of the most widely used email services. Creating a Google account is free and gives you immediate access to Gmail. Once your account is set up, you can explore Gmail’s features, adjust your settings, and manage your contacts. Gmail is known for being secure, easy to use, and accessible from almost any device. Gmail includes several helpful features that make communication more efficient. One major feature is spam filtering. Gmail automatically moves suspicious or unwanted messages into a separate spam folder, and anything in that folder is deleted after 30 days. Another feature is conversation view, which groups related messages together so you can read them like a text message thread. Gmail also offers built‑in chat options, including instant messaging, voice chat, and video chat. The phone‑calling feature allows you to call any phone number in the world, and calls within the United States are free. Understanding email begins with knowing how it compares to traditional mail. Email is faster, easier to organize, and accessible anywhere at any time. It includes productivity tools, privacy features, and the ability to communicate with multiple people at once. Email addresses follow a simple format, such as username@gmail.com, and different providers offer different services. If you use Google, your email address will be a Gmail address. Webmail providers allow you to access your email from any device with an internet connection, while school or work email systems may use different platforms. Email applications help users manage and organize their messages more efficiently. It is also important to recognize and avoid email scams. You should never send money to someone just because they emailed you. Spam, scams, and phishing attempts continue to evolve, but learning what to look for helps keep your inbox and computer safe. Activity 2 Sending email is one of the basic skills every user should know. You can send a simple message or customize it by changing the font, attaching files, or adding a signature. The compose window includes fields for recipients, CC and BCC, the subject line, the message body, and the send button. You can also save a signature that will automatically appear on every message you send. Responding to email is just as important. When you reply to a message, you can check any attachments, star important messages, and forward messages when needed. Unread messages appear in your inbox, and you can see the sender’s name and preview the content. Clicking the arrow next to a message shows more details. Starring messages helps you keep track of important information. Managing email includes deleting unwanted messages, archiving messages, applying labels, adding filters, and using Gmail’s search tools. Deleting unnecessary messages helps prevent your storage from filling up. Reporting spam helps Gmail improve its filtering system. Archiving allows you to hide messages without deleting them, so you can access them later. Labels help you organize your messages into categories such as “Travel,” “Work,” or “School,” and you can even change the label colors. Gmail is also easy to use on mobile devices. You can set up Gmail in your phone’s built‑in email app or use the official Gmail app for iPhone or Android. This makes it simple to send and receive messages on the go. Activity 3 Mozilla Thunderbird is a free, open‑source personal information manager operated by MZLA Technologies Corporation, a subsidiary of the Mozilla Foundation. Thunderbird includes features such as news feeds and instant messaging. Additional features can be added through extensions, which are installed using XPInstall modules. Thunderbird offers strong message‑management tools, including support for multiple identities within one account and system‑specific new‑email notifications. It also includes spam filtering and supports various file formats through plugins. Thunderbird Pro provides enhanced functionality for users who need more advanced features.
